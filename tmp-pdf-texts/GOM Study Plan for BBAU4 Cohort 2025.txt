Minimum Credits Required
GE: 4/5 GE courses + 2 College-Specified Courses (18/21 CU)
GE: 2 ENGL + CCIV + GE1601 (10 CU)
College: 11 College Cores + 3/4 College Electives (42/45 CU)
Major: 10 Major courses (30 CU)
Minor + Free Electives: 6 courses (18 CU)
Min. Total: 41 courses (121 CUs)
Max. Allowable: 48 courses (144 CUs)
Note:
(1) 	English Language Requirement:
Students admitted with HKDSE English Level 3 or equivalent are required to complete the 6-credit course LC0200A and LC0200B English
for Academic Purposes (EAP) prior to taking the 2 GE English courses (GE1401 and GE2402).
(2) 	Gateway Education Course Distributional Requirements
Take at least 3 credits from each of the 3 areas:
Area 1: Arts and Humanities
Area 2: Study of Societies, Social and Business Organizations
Area 3: Science and Technology
(3) 	Chinese Language Requirement
Students scoring below Level 4 in HKDSE Chinese Language, or scoring below Grade D in HKALE AS-level Chinese Language and Culture are
required to take a 3-credit unit course CHIN1001 University Chinese I.
(4) 	Students who do not choose a minor are required to take Free Electives.
