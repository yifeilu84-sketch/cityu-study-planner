Why Should You Study this
Programme?
There is an increasing number of legal tasks in which a detailed knowledge of mathematics is a significant
asset to lawyers. The law-math double degree programme therefore fills an important market need in the
legal profession.
The training provided by this double degree programme will bring additional mathematical knowledge to
law students and equip them with critical skills in areas such as probability and statistics, quantitative
analysis and formal logic that are becoming increasingly important in the legal world. The mathematical and
computational skills that the graduates acquire in this double degree will give them an advantage in several
legal fields.

Enquiries
The law component of this double degree covers the required legal units and entitles graduates who wish to
practice law in Hong Kong to apply for a position in the Postgraduate Certificate in Laws (PCLL) programme
which we offer at City University.
