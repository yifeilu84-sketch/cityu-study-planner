Free Electives / Minor (optional)
Courses that can be used to satisfy this requirement are:
1. Any B-level courses approved by the University for un-restricted registration (not offered by the Department of
Mathematics).
2. Programme Electives in the BSCM curriculum.
Students with a home major may choose to declare a minor, which is optional, in accordance with the procedures
announced by the University. A minor requires 15 to 18 credit units. Credits earned to fulfill the minor requirement cannot
be used toward meeting the requirement for a major and/or other minor(s) taken by the student.
