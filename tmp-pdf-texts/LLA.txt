    Notes       1.   N ormative 4 - year degree students are expected to complete the College requirement courses  (2  courses/  6  credit  units)  and  some  University  requirement  and  Gateway  Education  requirement courses in their first year of studies. For an overview of degree require ments,  please refer to page 1 of this document and DegreeWorks.         2.   Students must take at least one and at most two of the core elective courses in order to  graduate: LT3199 Professional Internship I (3 credits), LT4235 Project (3 credits), LT4391  Undergra duate Research Project (6 credits).        3.   Under normal circumstances, Year 2 students can take elective courses at levels B2 and B3.  Year 3 students can take elective courses at levels B2, B3 and B4 while Year 4 students can  only take elective courses at leve ls B3 and B4.        4.   All students are required to take at least 4 major electives (12 credit units) at B4 level for  graduation.        5.   Do not change the courses pre - registered unnecessarily since they are offered once a year  only. Registration priority for B4 courses will be given to the graduating students first and  other cohorts later and so be patient and wait for your turn.        6.   Some el ective courses, e.g., B4 level courses, are offered every other year, so plan ahead.  For details of the course offering schedule, please contact your Major Leader.
