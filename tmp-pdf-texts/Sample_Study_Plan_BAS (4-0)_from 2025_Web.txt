Additional Information
*Students are required to take LC0200A and LC0200B (6 credit units for the two courses or 3 credit units for LC0200A with
early exit arrangement) and CHIN1001 University Chinese I (3 credit units) if they do not meet the respective threshold
requirement. The credit units are excluded from the minimum requirement for graduation but are counted in maximum credit
units allowed.
** Students of the CREATE Stream are required to register SM2724A/B/C/D/E/F (1 credit unit per course) in any three
semesters during the entire study period.
^Students may select 2 out of 6 Art and Science Studio courses, each of 6 credit units, up to a total of 12 credit units. Extra Art
and Science Studio courses taken, if any, will be counted towards fulfilment of Major Electives.
^^ Students may select Gateway Education, Free Elective or Minor Course to fulfil the School-Specified Gateway Education
Course Requirement.
% Students are required to complete at least one Art and Science Studio before taking SM4712C Graduation Thesis (GT) / Project.
